At a glance

Every recipe in the book, by how long it takes and how many ingredients it needs. Pick a dot to open the recipe.

Each dot is one recipe. Time is prep plus cooking, on a stretched scale so the quick dishes spread out. Dots are nudged slightly so recipes with the same numbers do not hide each other. The weeknight corner is 30 minutes or less and no more than 7 ingredients. The same recipes are listed in the chapters below and in the index.
