Crisis Relief

Emergency response in war zones, disasters, and displacement crises, including Gaza, Sudan, and Ukraine.

01World Central Kitchen

Worldwide · Emergency meals

Gets fresh meals to crisis zones faster than almost anyone else, from Gaza to US hurricane response. Founded by chef José Andrés.

Donate·Learn more

02Doctors Without Borders

Worldwide · Emergency medical care

Independent medical teams working in active war zones, including Gaza, Sudan, and Ukraine, often after other NGOs have pulled out.

Donate·Learn more

03UNRWA USA

Gaza & West Bank · Refugee relief

The most direct channel to UN relief operations for Palestine refugees: food, shelter, healthcare, and education across Gaza and the West Bank.

Donate·Learn more

04Anera

Gaza, West Bank, Lebanon, Jordan · Humanitarian aid

American Near East Refugee Aid: smaller and more regional than most, delivering medical supplies, food, and education support directly.

Donate·Learn more

05International Rescue Committee

Worldwide · Refugee resettlement

One of the largest relief networks, covering refugee crises from first emergency aid through long-term resettlement. Founded in 1933 at Albert Einstein's request.

Donate·Learn more

06UNICEF USA

Worldwide · Child emergency aid

UN-backed emergency aid for children in war zones and disaster areas: clean water, nutrition, vaccines, shelter. The safest, least improvisational choice here.

Donate·Learn more

07Mercy Corps

Worldwide · Disaster recovery

Pairs emergency relief with longer-term recovery: cash assistance and rebuilding livelihoods after the news cycle moves on.

Donate·Learn more

08CARE

Worldwide · Women & girls in crisis

Global humanitarian response built around women and girls, who are disproportionately affected in almost every crisis it responds to.

Donate·Learn more

09Save the Children

Worldwide · Child protection

Protects children in war zones and disaster areas: education continuity, trauma support, and basic survival needs.

Donate·Learn more

10Direct Relief

Worldwide · Medical supply delivery

Ships medicine directly to health workers in disaster zones, no markup, no politics. Your donation becomes medicine, not cash.

Donate·Learn more

Hunger & Poverty

Long-term food security and direct poverty relief beyond any single crisis.

01Action Against Hunger

Worldwide · Malnutrition treatment

Treats and prevents malnutrition in crisis zones, with a strong focus on children under five, as the medical emergency it actually is.

Donate·Learn more

02GiveDirectly

Sub-Saharan Africa · Direct cash transfers

Sends unconditional cash transfers straight to people in extreme poverty, no programs in between. The pick if you're skeptical of how charities spend money.

Donate·Learn more

03Oxfam

Worldwide · Poverty & policy advocacy

Pairs hunger and disaster response with policy advocacy aimed at the systems that keep poverty in place.

Donate·Learn more

04The Hunger Project

Africa, S. Asia, Latin America · Community-led development

Grassroots, community-led programs aimed at ending hunger for good, deliberately slower than repeat emergency aid.

Donate·Learn more

05World Food Program USA

Worldwide · Food assistance

Supports the UN's food-assistance operation, the largest on Earth, reaching people in famine and near-famine conditions.

Donate·Learn more

06Feeding America

United States · Domestic food banks

The largest domestic hunger-relief network in the US: local food banks that move food where it's actually needed.

Donate·Learn more

07Heifer International

Worldwide · Livestock & livelihoods

Gives families livestock, training, and market access to generate their own income, aiming to end the need for aid.

Donate·Learn more

08Concern Worldwide US

Worldwide · Extreme hunger response

Works in the hardest-to-reach places experiencing extreme hunger, often where few large organizations operate.

Donate·Learn more

09Bread for the World

United States · Hunger policy advocacy

An advocacy operation, not a service org, pushing US policy toward ending hunger.

Donate·Learn more

10Rise Against Hunger

Worldwide · Meal packaging

Runs volunteer meal-packaging events shipped to schools and crisis regions. The most hands-on option here, you can pack the boxes yourself.

Donate·Learn more

Animals & Wildlife

Rescue, shelter, and protection work for animals across the US and worldwide.

01ASPCA

United States · Anti-cruelty & rescue

Anti-cruelty investigations, disaster rescue, and shelter support from one of the oldest and most active animal welfare groups in the US.

Donate·Learn more

02Wildlife Conservation Society

Worldwide · Field conservation

Runs field conservation programs worldwide alongside the Bronx Zoo, one of the oldest conservation organizations in the country.

Donate·Learn more

03Defenders of Wildlife

United States · Species protection

Fights in court and Congress for native US wildlife, focused on the legal and policy battles that determine endangered species protections.

Donate·Learn more

04The Humane Society of the US

Broad-spectrum animal protection: disaster rescue, cruelty investigations, and legislative campaigns against puppy mills and factory farming.

Donate·Learn more

05World Wildlife Fund

Habitat and species conservation at a global scale, from anti-poaching work to protecting the ecosystems endangered animals depend on.

Donate·Learn more

06World Animal Protection

Worldwide · Disaster & farm welfare

Donate·Learn more

07Best Friends Animal Society

United States · No-kill shelters

Leads the no-kill shelter movement in the US: direct rescue work plus the policy push to end animal homelessness nationwide.

08Farm Sanctuary

United States · Farm animal rescue

Rescues farm animals from cruelty and neglect and gives them a real home, paired with advocacy against factory farming.

Donate·Learn more

09Mercy For Animals

Worldwide · Factory farming reform

Investigates and campaigns against factory farming cruelty, going undercover inside the operations most others can't see.

Donate·Learn more

10Oceana

Worldwide · Ocean conservation

The largest organization focused solely on ocean conservation: overfishing, marine habitat, and the animals that live entirely underwater.
