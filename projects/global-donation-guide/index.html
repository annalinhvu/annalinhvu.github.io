A global donation guide

A personal, non-ranked guide to organizations working in crisis relief, hunger and poverty, children's health, and animals and conservation.

Built with US-based donors in mind; several organizations link to their US affiliate. Tax treatment and donation availability may differ if you're giving from outside the US.

How this guide is reviewed

Selected based on active programs, transparent public information, and coverage by independent charity evaluators, then checked against official sources. Last reviewed July 2026. Inclusion is not a financial, legal, or effectiveness rating.
